# Privacy summary

Nuke Unit Tracker stores user-entered bets, unit values, notes, and slip photos in the app’s local data store. If the user enables iCloud, that private data syncs through the user’s private iCloud database. It is not sent to the Nuke community service.

The community service stores public posts mirrored from the configured Nuke Free Picks Discord channel and device notification tokens/preferences necessary to deliver free-pick notifications. It does not collect sportsbook credentials, wagers, or payment information.

Before App Store submission, publish a complete privacy policy at a stable HTTPS URL and complete Apple’s App Privacy questionnaire using the production implementation.
