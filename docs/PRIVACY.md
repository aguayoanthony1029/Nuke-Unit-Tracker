# Nuke Unit Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Nuke Unit Tracker is a personal record-keeping app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

## Information you enter

The app lets you enter a unit value and records about sports-betting activity, including titles, sports, leagues, sportsbooks, bet types, odds, risk units, results, dates, notes, parlay legs, and optional slip photos. If you choose **Scan a slip to prefill**, Apple’s on-device text-recognition framework reads text from that user-selected image to suggest record details for you to review.

You do not create an account with Nuke Unit Tracker. The app does not request your name, email address, phone number, sportsbook password, payment information, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

## Storage and iCloud

Tracker records are stored on your device. If iCloud is available for the app, Apple may sync those records through your private iCloud database so they are available on your devices. Nuke Sports Bets does not receive those records through a developer-operated server.

Slip photos are reduced to a maximum 1,280-pixel dimension, compressed, and stored only inside the app’s private storage on the device where you selected them. The app caps local slip-photo storage at 400 MB and never automatically deletes a photo to make room. Files use iOS file protection while the device is locked. Slip text recognition happens on the device; neither the image nor recognized text is uploaded by the current Nuke Unit Tracker app. If you used an earlier version that saved a slip photo to your private iCloud database, the app may retrieve that existing image so it remains available after an update. Slip photos are not included in CSV exports.

Apple processes iCloud data under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

## Information we do not collect

Nuke Unit Tracker has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, or developer-operated backend. The app does not sell personal information and does not track you across apps or websites.

## External links

The app contains optional links that open in your browser:

- Whop, for a separate optional Nuke Sports Bets community membership
- Discord, reached outside the app through that community
- YouTube, for an educational bankroll-management video
- National Council on Problem Gambling, for responsible-gambling resources
- GitHub, for this privacy policy and support

The Whop and Discord community is separate from Nuke Unit Tracker. An optional community membership does not unlock app content or features. A membership purchase occurs entirely in Whop’s browser, where Whop presents its current price, renewal terms, cancellation options, and any discount eligibility. Once you leave the app, the external service’s own privacy policy and terms apply. Nuke Unit Tracker does not send your tracker records or slip photos to those services.

## Retention and deletion

Records stay on your device until you delete individual bets or use **You → Delete all app data**. Deleting all app data removes the local profile, tracker records, parlay legs, and slip photos. When iCloud sync is active, deletions of synced tracker records are also sent to your private iCloud database. Apple may retain backups according to its own policies.

## Age and intended audience

Nuke Unit Tracker is intended only for adults age 18 and older. It is not directed to children. The app does not accept deposits, connect to sportsbook accounts, place or transmit wagers, or award prizes.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store listing as appropriate.
